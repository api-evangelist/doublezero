# DoubleZero

> DoubleZero is a protocol for building and operating high-performance, permissionless networks — a global dedicated-fiber network for distributed systems like blockchains, delivering low-latency connectivity and real-time data infrastructure where milliseconds matter.

## Products

- [DoubleZero Edge](https://doublezero.xyz/dz-edge): Dedicated, low-latency transport layer for Solana market data.
- [Geolocation Verification](https://doublezero.xyz/geolocation-verification): Physics-based, onchain-verifiable location proof (experimental).
